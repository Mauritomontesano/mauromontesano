About

Mauro Montesano is a business consultant focused on financial services, with nearly twenty years of international experience — most recently in fintech, and before that in iGaming, insurance and banking. He has held senior roles spanning risk management, corporate finance and executive leadership, including Chief Financial Officer of a leading Malta-based iGaming group, Risk Management Solvency II project lead at Accenture, and Corporate Finance consultant at KPMG.

His current independent practice focuses on business planning (including support in obtaining bank financing), business valuation, financial modeling, capital allocation and AI financial systems for corporate and start-up clients. He combines regulatory expertise with hands-on experience building automated financial and trading tools.
